A Few Things You Might Wonder.

Everything you need to know about our sourcing, brewing, and delivery.

Matcha is shaded Japanese green tea leaves (tencha) gently milled on granite stone wheels into micro-fine powder. Because you drink the ground whole leaf suspended in water or milk, you consume all nutrients, antioxidants (EGCG), and L-theanine directly.
Ceremonial matcha is made exclusively from tender first-harvest spring leaves, resulting in a gentle, sweet, rich umami taste intended to be enjoyed neat with water. Culinary grade uses later harvests with a bolder, more astringent profile designed to shine through baked goods and blended lattes.
Sift 2g (approx. 1 tsp) of matcha powder into a bowl. Add 60ml of hot water at 80°C (175°F). Vigorously whisk back and forth in an 'M' or 'W' motion using a bamboo whisk (chasen) for 20 seconds until a creamy layer of jade micro-foam develops.
Keep your matcha sealed airtight in its original tin or an opaque container inside the refrigerator. Light, air, and humidity oxidize the green chlorophyll. For optimum flavor and vibrancy, enjoy within 6 to 8 weeks of opening.
